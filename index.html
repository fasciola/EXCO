About EXCO GEOVISTA

From the sky to decision-ready data.

EXCO GEOVISTA combines drone platforms, high-resolution sensors and geospatial analysis to support mining, quarrying and geological survey teams across challenging terrain.

Our focus is simple: capture more site context with less field exposure, transform it into practical mapping deliverables, and make complex ground conditions easier to understand.
